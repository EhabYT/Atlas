=========================
Liquid Glass
=========================

Liquid Glass is a transparency-focused visual style for Windows,
giving the desktop, taskbar and windows a frosted glass-like
appearance.

These scripts enable or disable the Windows transparency and
accent colour effects that the style relies on:
 - Transparency effects (EnableTransparency)
 - Accent colour on surfaces (ColorPrevalence, DWM)

For the full Liquid Glass taskbar look, see the 'Taskbar Styling'
folder, which uses Windhawk and the Windows 11 Taskbar Styling
Guide.

For the matching wallpaper and theme, see the 'EBOS Liquid Glass'
theme in the Personalisation settings.
