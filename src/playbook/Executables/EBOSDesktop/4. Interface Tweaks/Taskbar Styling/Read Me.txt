=========================
Taskbar Styling
=========================

Taskbar styling in EBOS is done with Windhawk, a safe and open
source mod marketplace for Windows.

How to get the Liquid Glass taskbar:
 1. Install Windhawk (see 'Install Windhawk').
 2. Open the Windows 11 Taskbar Styling Guide.
 3. In the Windhawk 'Windows 11 Taskbar Styler' mod settings,
    paste one of the themes from the guide, such as the
    Liquid Glass theme (see 'Open Liquid Glass Taskbar Theme').

To revert, remove the theme from the mod settings or uninstall
Windhawk.
